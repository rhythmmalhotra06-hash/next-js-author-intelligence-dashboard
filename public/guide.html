One score hides everything. So we don't use one.

The thing to watch for

When the numbers disagree — that's the gold.

A great rating with no replay views? Students were entertained but didn't transform.
A low rating in a slot that always fails? It's the slot, not the speaker.

The dashboard flags these moments for you. Most of your best decisions will start there.
